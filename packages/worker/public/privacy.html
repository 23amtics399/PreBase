Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Welcome to PreBase. This Privacy Policy applies to the PreBase Minimum Viable Product (MVP) and describes how we handle information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect account information (email and password) to authenticate users. We also collect the text and file content ("Knowledge Base") that you explicitly upload to construct your AI chatbots.

2. Use of Information

Your uploaded knowledge base is solely used to generate responses via our AI models for the bots you configure. It is not used to train our AI models or third-party AI models.

3. Important Restrictions on Uploaded Data

Do NOT upload sensitive information. PreBase is currently in MVP. You must explicitly ensure that you do not upload passwords, authentication credentials, financial records, medical records, or any other confidential personal or corporate data into the system.

4. Widget Usage and Chat Logs

When visitors use your embedded PreBase widgets, their chat inputs are temporarily processed to generate responses. We may log these requests for security, rate-limiting, and abuse prevention purposes.

5. Third-Party Services

PreBase utilizes Cloudflare infrastructure and Cloudflare Workers AI (specifically IBM Granite models) to operate and process inferences. Your data is processed in accordance with Cloudflare's security and privacy practices.

6. Contact

For questions or requests regarding your data, please contact the repository maintainer.
