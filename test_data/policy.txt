CORPORATE IT POLICY - ACME CORP
Effective: Jan 1, 2026

1. Acceptable Use
Company-provided laptops are for professional use only. Personal browsing is permitted during designated break times, provided it does not violate the code of conduct.

2. Password Requirements
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and include a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. Passwords must be changed every 90 days.

3. Remote Access
Employees working remotely must connect via the corporate VPN. Connecting to public, unsecured Wi-Fi networks without the VPN is strictly prohibited and grounds for disciplinary action.

4. Hardware Return
Upon termination of employment, all hardware must be returned to the IT department within 3 business days. Failure to return hardware will result in a deduction from the final paycheck equivalent to the depreciated value of the equipment.
