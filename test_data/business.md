# Luigi's Pizza

## Hours of Operation
- Monday: Closed
- Tuesday - Thursday: 11:00 AM - 9:00 PM
- Friday - Saturday: 11:00 AM - 10:30 PM
- Sunday: 12:00 PM - 8:00 PM

## Location
We are located at 123 Main Street, Springfield. Parking is available in the rear lot.

## Menu Highlights
Our signature dish is the **Truffle Mushroom Pizza**, featuring wild mushrooms, truffle oil, and fresh mozzarella on our sourdough crust.
We also offer gluten-free crusts for an additional $2.

## Reservations
We do not take reservations. Seating is strictly on a first-come, first-served basis. For parties of 10 or more, please call ahead.
